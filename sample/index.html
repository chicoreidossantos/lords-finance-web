Coverage

The reserve divided by the SOLIDUS in circulation: $2,740,000 ÷ 640,000 = 4.28. The reserve holds $4.28 of ETH for every SOLIDUS. Each threshold changes what the protocol allows.

  • At or below 1.2 · StopIf coverage touches 1.2 at a batch price, the series stops. Each SOLIDUS becomes a claim on $0.95 of ETH, the Lords keep the rest, and a new series opens.
  • 1.2 to 1.3 · Combined mints onlySOLIDUS can be minted only with a combined order, which brings at least $2 of ETH per SOLIDUS and always raises coverage. Redemptions and proportional exits stay open.
  • 1.3 to 2 · Mints wait one hourThe part of a mint or net withdrawal that would take coverage below 2 waits one hour, and nothing can take it below 1.3. SOLIDUS holders receive 10% of their fair share of the income.
  • 1.45 to 1.55 · Default automatic redemptionA wallet that never set a level is redeemed automatically at its own default level, somewhere in this band.
  • 2 to 3 · Fast mintsMints settle at the next price round. The SOLIDUS holders’ part of their fair share rises from 10% to 100%.
  • 3 and above · Full shareSOLIDUS holders receive their full fair share of the income: per dollar, the same as the Lords.

If coverage touches 1.2 at a batch price, the series stops: each SOLIDUS becomes a claim on $0.95 of ETH at that round’s price, the Lords keep the rest of the reserve, and a new series opens at one dollar under the next name. After the twentieth, the system ends.

Coverage is checked only at the prices that settle batches. With no orders, the next check can come hours later, and the payout can then be below $0.95. Anyone can ask for a check by placing a verification order.

Dukes (DUKES) The reward token

DUKES holders receive 10% of all the protocol’s income, paid in Florins, for as long as the protocol exists. DUKES carry no vote and no other right.

  • Fixed supply: 100,000,000 DUKES

    All created at deployment. No more can ever be made.

  • Launch-cap prize: 5,000,000

    Shared evenly over the 90 days of the cap, 55,556 a day, among SOLIDUS holders and Lords. In the 30 days for Lords only, all of it went to Lords. So far: 2,722,222.

  • Ramp after the cap: 45,000,000

    Over the 730 days after the cap, from 123,288 a day down to zero in a straight line.

  • Team: 50,000,000

    The other half of the supply.

  • How each day is shared

    By the same fair-share rule as the income, dollar by dollar and by the time each balance is held. Today: 0.0203 DUKES a day per dollar, for SOLIDUS and Florins alike.

Before you place an order

  • The price comes later. An order settles at the next Chainlink ETH/USD round observed at least 60 s after its block, not at the moment you place it. Chainlink publishes when ETH moves 0.5%, or once an hour. From 2 to 18 October 2026 that wait would have averaged 28.6 minutes. During the wait you stay exposed to ETH.
  • One price source. Chainlink ETH/USD is the protocol’s only price, with nothing to check it against. A wrong round, or an action of the feed’s owner, can fire a false stop, prevent a stop or halt settlement.
  • Until the cap ends, in 41 days in this example, SOLIDUS is not yet a stablecoin: it could be worth more than one dollar.
  • During the cap the code is new, and you can lose your money.
  • During the cap, if the projected coverage is below your automatic-redemption level (by default between 1.45 and 1.55), your SOLIDUS is redeemed at $0.999 even if it is worth more in the market. If you buy above $0.999, set your level before you buy.

Automatic redemption level

When the projected coverage falls to your level, any bot can redeem your SOLIDUS for you at $0.999. Its fee is capped at 1% of the amount and by the cost of gas.

This wallet never set a level. Its default, 1.4873, comes from its address; defaults run from 1.45 to 1.55.

During the cap, if the projected coverage is below your level, your SOLIDUS is redeemed at $0.999 even if it is worth more in the market. If you buy above $0.999, set your level before you buy.

Setting any level, even the default value, ends the default for this wallet, in every series. If liquid ETH runs out while coverage falls through 1.55 to 1.45, wallets with the highest default levels are redeemed earlier and take the liquid ETH; wallets with the lowest can be paid in wstETH.
