Coverage

The reserve divided by the SOLIDUS in circulation: $2,740,000 ÷ 640,000 = 4.28. The reserve holds $4.28 of ETH for every SOLIDUS. It is measured once per batch, at the batch price, after the Lords’ entries and the combined orders, and that one figure rules the whole batch.

Past 7 batches

SOLIDUS holders receive their part by rebalancing: each batch, balances are adjusted to pass on that part. It is not interest, and it can be zero. In these 7 batches it added 0.000319 SOLIDUS to each SOLIDUS.

The Lords’ part stays in the reserve and raises the book value of a Florin. That value also moves with ETH, 1.30 times as much, and it can fall.

With coverage at 2 or more, the income after DUKES is shared in proportion to capital: today 23.4% to SOLIDUS and 76.6% to Lords. Below 2, the Lords’ part goes from 50% to 80%.

Dukes (DUKES) The reward token

DUKES holders share 10% of the protocol’s income, for as long as the protocol exists. DUKES carry no vote and no other right.

  • Fixed supply: 100,000,000 DUKES

    All created at deployment. No more can ever be made.

  • For participants: 50,000,000

    Each batch shares 1/730 of what is left, only among Florins, by capital, from the first batch. Batch 49 shared 64,131. So far: 3,248,156. Left: 46,751,844.

  • Team: 50,000,000

    To one team address, released over 4 years. Of these, 3,000,000 are publicly committed to bug rewards and 1,000,000 to the testnet; that split is a public commitment, not part of the code.

Before you place an order

  • The price comes later. An order waits for the daily cutoff at 00:00 UTC and settles at the first Chainlink ETH/USD round after it, not at the moment you place it. That can be almost a day. During the wait you stay exposed to ETH. If no round comes, the order moves to the next batch.
  • One price source. Chainlink ETH/USD is the protocol’s only price, with nothing to check it against. A wrong round settles the whole batch at that price and can set off a conversion. The feed’s owners can change it without notice.
  • Conversion. In a batch that opens with coverage at 1.2 or less, one third of every SOLIDUS balance is converted into ETH at $0.99, less below coverage 1.
  • Exit box. If it leaves, you hold USDT, which its issuer can freeze, until coverage is back at 2.
  • Until the cap ends, in 41 batches in this example, SOLIDUS is not yet a stablecoin: it could trade above one dollar.
  • During the cap the code is new, and you can lose your money.
